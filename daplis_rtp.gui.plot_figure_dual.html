daplis_rtp.gui.plot_figure_dual module

Plot canvas for the full-sensor (dual-board) tab.

512 pixels on the x-axis. A dashed boundary line is always drawn at x=255.5 to mark the split between the two sensor halves. When grouping is enabled, vertical lines are drawn at 64, 128, 192, 320, 384, 448.
