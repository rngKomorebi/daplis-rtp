presetmask_pixels()[source]

Apply each board’s recorded warm-pixel mask, or lift them.

Each board uses the shared daughterboard and its own motherboard to look up its file. A board with no mask recorded is skipped silently; the warning comes only when neither has one, since one board masked and the other not is a perfectly ordinary state.

refresh()[source]

report_occupancy(fw_ver, occupancy_1, occupancy_2)[source]

Hand one refresh’s slot occupancy to the status box.

The pixels of interest are given on the 0-511 x-axis of this tab, so each board takes the part of them that falls on its own half and maps it to TDCs with the pix_add_fix its rates were built with - always off for board 1 and on for board 2.

presetmask_pixels()[source]

Apply the recorded warm-pixel mask, or lift it again.

The file is looked up from the daughterboard and motherboard chosen above; a combination that has no mask recorded says so and clears the tick rather than leaving it set over nothing.

report_occupancy(occupancy, fw_ver, pix_add_fix)[source]

Hand one refresh’s slot occupancy to the status box.

The pixels of interest come either from the box’s own field or from the mask and the x-limits; they are pixel numbers as plotted, so they are mapped to TDCs with the same pix_add_fix the rates were built with.

slot_mask_changed()[source]

Rebuild the mask, update the count, and redraw.

Redrawing here is what makes a tick visible at once: the rates are kept unmasked, so re-applying the mask to them is the whole of the work, and it costs one redraw rather than a re-read of the file.

refresh()[source]

Read the newest file and rebuild the histogram.

There is no stream on this tab: a histogram is something to look at, not something to watch move. The button reads the newest file every time it is pressed, so a run in progress is followed by pressing it again.
