daplis_rtp.gui.tabs.mzi module

MZI: the photon rate in two pixels, against time.

The two arms of a Mach-Zehnder interferometer land on two pixels, and what is being watched is how their counts move against each other as the interferometer drifts or is driven. One point is added per data file, so the trace grows at whatever rate the acquisition writes.

Ordered {heading: 1-D array}; every column the same length.
